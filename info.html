How to Play

Game Overview

El Pollo Loco is a jump and run adventure, that currently features 3 Levels, with a Bossfight on Level 3.

Fight your way through the Levels by jumping on top of the enemies or collecting salsa bottles and throwing them on your foes.

Save up some bottles for the Boss, otherwise try to jump on it during the charge.

Collection System

There are currently 2 collectables in the game, salsa bottles and coins.

Sasla bottles: Max. amount = 5. Throwables to defeat your enemies.

Coins: Max. amount = 25. Progress is shown in amounts of 5.
